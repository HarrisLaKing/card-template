# A free digital business card you own

A card page, a "save my contact" file and an email signature, hosted free on GitHub Pages.
You own the link, so the QR code you print never breaks and never costs a fee.

From Hold the Door. The full how-to is the guide "Own Your Business Card".

## Make your own (about 20 minutes)

1. Click **Use this template** → **Create a new repository**. Name it `card`. Keep it **Public**.
2. Open **`info.js`**, click the pencil, and change the words in quote marks to yours. Click **Commit changes**.
3. Open **`contact.vcf`** and do the same (name, title, phone, email, your card's address).
4. Upload your photo as **`photo.jpg`** (Add file → Upload files). It replaces the sample.
5. **Settings → Pages →** Branch `main`, folder `/ (root)` → **Save**. In 1–2 minutes your card is at
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/card/`.
6. Open your card in Chrome on a computer. **⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Create QR code → Download.**
   Rename the file `qr.png` and upload it here.
7. Open `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/card/signature.html`, copy the white box, and paste it into
   your Gmail or Outlook signature settings.

## Good to know

- Change `info.js` and the card page updates by itself. A **pasted signature is a copy**: paste it again.
- Gmail: turn off **Plain text mode** (in a new email, ⋮), or the signature turns into plain words.
- Don't need a part? Leave `signupLink` as `""`, or delete a group from `info.js`.

Free to copy and change.
